# Samurai sprite sources

The silhouettes in `kendo.svg` and `ronin.svg` are traced frame by frame from these free clips:

- Kendo practitioner (tool-page samurai): Pixabay video 315583, https://pixabay.com/videos/kendo-shinai-teacher-japanese-315583/
- Back-view ronin in mist (home hero, 404): Pixabay video 339449, https://pixabay.com/videos/ninja-warrior-weapon-samurai-339449/
