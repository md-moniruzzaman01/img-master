# QA 2026-11-08: e2e brought in line with the Ink Samurai design, full rerun, Lighthouse

## Summary
- mobile-chromium full suite (389 tests, 2 workers, 36 min): **368 passed, 20 failed, 1 skipped**. The 20 failures reproduce alone (rerun with `--last-failed`: 20/20 fail again), so none is flaky. Flaky list: none.
- 1 of the 20 is the expected placeholder-domain test (`seo.spec.ts` "the site URL is a real domain"), left alone.
- 19 are real product gaps (below). 0 are stale tests.
- mobile-webkit smoke (resize, privacy, mobile-ux picker-fold/visible): 34 passed, 2 skipped (chromium-only), 0 failed.
- Lighthouse (mobile preset, 3 runs, median): **Performance 0.49-0.54 on all 7 audited pages, gate is 0.95. Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO are 1.00 on all 7.** See P1.
- Verdict: **don't ship** until P1 (482 preloaded font files) is fixed. The rest are Medium or lower.
