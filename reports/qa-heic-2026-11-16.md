## 3. Privacy (hard rule): PASS

Chromium and WebKit, both pages, 2 HEIC files converted plus ZIP download:
- 44 requests (Chromium), 42 (WebKit): all same-origin GET (`/_next/static/...`, RSC `__next.*.txt`, `/manifest.webmanifest`) or `blob:`/`data:`. No POST/PUT/PATCH, no request body, no third-party host.
- Zero CSP violations (`securitypolicyviolation` listener and console errors), with `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` allowed by the production CSP.
- libheif chunk (largest file in `_next/static/chunks`, about 2 MB) is requested exactly once, only after picking a HEIC; **not** requested on page load, and **not** requested converting JPG/PNG/WebP on `/webp-to-jpg`. A HEIC picked on `/webp-to-jpg` (Chromium) fetches it then, and converts.
- The chunk name is not present in the HTML of `/`, `/heic-to-jpg`, `/heic-to-png`, `/webp-to-jpg`, `/compress-image-to-50kb`.
