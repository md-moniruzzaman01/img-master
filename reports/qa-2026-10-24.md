## 3. What passed (details)

- **ZIP, every mode, both browsers** (text, emoji, image, and text and emoji reached from the hub): the file list equals PRODUCT.md section 4.4 exactly (`favicon.svg` only for text and emoji). `favicon.ico`: reserved 0, type 1, count 3, entries 16/32/48, planes 1, 32 bpp, offsets contiguous with no trailing bytes, every embedded image has a PNG signature and matching IHDR size. PNG files are exactly 16, 32, 180, 192, 512 (IHDR read, and decoded in the browser). `site.webmanifest` parses, lists 192 and 512 icons that exist in the ZIP, name equals the text. `README.txt` contains every snippet line, and every `href` in the snippet exists in the ZIP. The ZIP is named `favicon-package.zip`; "Download favicon.ico only" is byte-identical to the ICO in the ZIP.
- **Text:** 1 to 3 characters kept, 4th dropped, spaces trimmed. Bengali "আ" and "আমি" render (239 and 335 distinct colours in Chromium and WebKit) and land in the SVG. Empty and whitespace-only disable ZIP and ICO downloads and show the hint (see QA-12 for the stale preview).
- **Emoji:** pasting two emoji keeps the newest; ZWJ family, flag and heart with variation selector count as one; quick picks work; emptying disables downloads.
- **Image:** picker, zoom, padding, shape and transparent switch change output as expected. Transparent PNG keeps alpha 0 when transparent is on; opaque (255, white corner) when off; circle corner has alpha 0. Not-an-image and corrupt JPG: error banner, no downloads, recovers with a good file. 40x40, 1000x8000 and 48 MP images all produce a package.
- **png-to-ico:** 300 px PNG gives 16/32/48/256; 40x20 gives 16/32/48 (header, offsets, PNG signatures checked); a transparent PNG keeps alpha in all four embedded PNGs; JPG input shows "This tool needs a PNG file. Convert your image to PNG first." with no download; a mixed batch of four is `partial` with the right two `.ico` files in "Download all"; 10 files give 10 cards and a ZIP of 10 unique names (`icon-1.ico`, `icon-1-2.ico`).
- **Hostile input:** `<&"`, `</t`, `<sc`, `">&`, `'<>`, `&lt`, `\u0`, `{{x`, `&#x` (the field cuts the brief's `<script>"&` and `</text><script>` to three characters; checked too). Each package passes the full check; the SVG parses with DOMParser, has no script, foreignObject or `on*` attributes, escapes `<` and `&`, and its text equals the typed text. Manifest and README stay intact. No new inline script, no off-origin script, no `onerror` element, `window.__pwn` stays 0. Tampered `localStorage` (script text in colour, weight, shape and text, padding 999) is ignored or clamped. A file named `"><img src=x onerror=...>.png` is shown as text and sanitised to `img-src-x-onerror-window-__pwn-1.ico` in the ZIP.
- **Preview:** raw CLS 0.0000 / 0.0040 / 0.0000 (text, hub, emoji) through a slider drag and three colour changes (limit 0.05). Object URLs: after 8 edits, 45 created, 40 revoked, 5 alive (one package); png-to-ico frees the old batch.
- **Mobile UX at 360x640:** first control, subtitle and header are all above the fold on the four pages. Bottom of the first control: hub picker 339 px, text field 349, emoji field 349, png-to-ico picker 265 (limit 640); subtitle ends at 179 px. No horizontal scroll in initial, result and mode-switched states. Tap targets in the tool area, header and breadcrumbs are all 48 px or more (mode tabs, emoji quick picks, colour inputs, sliders, switch included). Sticky bar: buttons 48 px or more, last footer link and snippet end above the bar at scroll end, every control receives the pointer when centred. Copy works: Chromium shows "Copied" and the clipboard equals the snippet (Windows adds CRLF, OS behaviour); WebKit also shows "Copied" (Playwright WebKit on Windows).
- **Accessibility:** axe (WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA plus best-practice), light and dark: all 23 pages and the 404, plus the hub empty, with results, text mode, invalid text, emoji mode, after copy, text page, emoji page, png-to-ico empty and with a failed card. Keyboard: mode tabs (arrows, wrap, Home, End), weight and shape radiogroups, slider arrows, Tab to Download ZIP, Enter downloads; the preview region has a name and description, preview images are decorative, "Preview updated" is announced politely.
- **SEO (four new pages):** one H1; titles 48-51 chars; descriptions 141-146 chars; all unique; canonical absolute without slash; OG and Twitter tags with 1200x630 images; WebApplication, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage parse and match the page (FAQ answers identical); internal links resolve; no banned claims. Words below the tool, excluding related cards: generator 560, text 519, emoji 489, png-to-ico 481 (minimum 400). Worst similarity involving the new pages: text vs emoji 9.3% containment (limit 30%).
